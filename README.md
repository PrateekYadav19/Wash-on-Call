# 🚗 WashAtDoor — AI-Powered Doorstep Vehicle Care

> **Your Vehicle. Your Place. Our Care.**

WashAtDoor is an **on-demand, AI-powered vehicle care platform** that brings professional vehicle washing and cleaning services directly to the customer's doorstep.

Instead of driving to a washing center and waiting for the vehicle to be cleaned, customers can simply book a service through the platform. A verified professional from a nearby washing partner comes to the customer's location, performs the selected service, and completes the booking.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Traditional vehicle washing requires customers to:

* Drive their vehicle to a washing center
* Wait for the service to finish
* Spend additional time travelling
* Search for a reliable washing provider
* Compare prices and service quality manually

This becomes especially inconvenient when someone needs their vehicle cleaned urgently before a party, meeting, trip, or other event.

---

The goal is to make WashAtDoor more than just another vehicle-washing marketplace.

The system can analyze:

* Dirt and dust

* Dents
* Wheel condition
* Tyre appearance
* Glass condition

# 💳 Payments

The platform is designed to support secure online payments using **Razorpay**.

Payment flow:

```text
Customer confirms booking
        ↓
Backend creates payment order
        ↓
Razorpay Checkout
        ↓
Customer completes payment
        ↓
Server verifies payment
        ↓
Booking confirmed
```

Sensitive Razorpay credentials must be stored securely using environment variables and must never be exposed in frontend code.
